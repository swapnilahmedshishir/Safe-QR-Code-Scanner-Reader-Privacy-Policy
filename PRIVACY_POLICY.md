# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026

**App Name:** Safe QR Code Scanner & Reader

This Privacy Policy explains how Safe QR Code Scanner & Reader ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing the information required to provide the App's QR code, barcode scanning, QR generation, and link safety features.

## 1. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, the App may request access to the following device features:

### Camera

The App may request camera permission to scan QR codes and barcodes.

Camera access is used only to provide scanning functionality. The App does not intentionally use the camera to identify you or continuously record video.

Camera access is activated only when you use a camera-based scanning feature.

### Photos / Images

If the App provides a gallery or image scanning feature, you may select an image from your device to decode a QR code or barcode.

The App processes the selected image for the requested scanning operation.

The App does not intentionally access your entire photo library for unrelated purposes.

### Device Storage

The App may store application data such as scan history, generated QR codes, preferences, or exported files on your device, depending on the features you use.

Where data is stored locally, it remains on your device unless you explicitly export, share, or otherwise transfer it.

## 2. QR Codes and Barcodes

When you scan a QR code or barcode, the App processes the scanned information to provide the requested functionality.

Depending on the type of code, the scanned content may include:

* Website URLs
* Text
* Product identifiers
* Contact information
* Wi-Fi information
* Telephone numbers
* SMS information
* Email information
* Other information encoded in the QR code or barcode

The App does not intentionally collect this information for advertising or sell scanned content to third parties.

## 3. Scan History

The App may provide a scan history feature.

When scan history is stored locally on your device, the information remains stored locally and is not automatically uploaded to our servers.

You may delete locally stored scan history through the App where such functionality is available.

If you use an export or sharing feature, the exported information may leave your device according to the destination you select.

## 4. QR Code Generation

The App allows you to generate QR codes from information that you voluntarily enter.

Depending on the QR type, this information may include:

* Website URLs
* Text
* Wi-Fi credentials
* Contact information
* Phone numbers
* SMS content
* Email addresses
* Social media links
* Other information you choose to encode

QR data entered for generation is used to create the QR code requested by you.

You should avoid entering sensitive information into a QR code unless you understand the consequences of sharing that QR code.

## 5. Smart Safety Preview / Quishing Protection

The App may inspect URLs contained in scanned QR codes before allowing you to open them.

The purpose of this feature is to help users identify potentially suspicious, misleading, phishing, scam, or otherwise unsafe links before visiting them.

The App may analyze information associated with a URL, such as:

* The URL itself
* Domain information
* Security-related indicators
* Other technical information required to evaluate the link

If the Smart Safety Preview feature uses a third-party security service or remote server, the URL or relevant technical information may be transmitted to that service for analysis.

The App does not guarantee that every malicious, phishing, scam, or unsafe website will be detected.

Users should always exercise caution before opening links or providing information to websites.

## 6. Internet Access

The App may use an internet connection for features that require online functionality, including URL safety analysis or other services that cannot be performed entirely on the device.

If a feature operates entirely on the device, the relevant information may remain on the device.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide certain functionality, such as URL security analysis, crash reporting, analytics, or other technical services.

If third-party services are used, those services may process limited technical or feature-related information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell users' personal information.

Any third-party service used by the App should be disclosed in the App's Google Play Data Safety declaration where required.

## 8. Advertising

If the App does not display advertising, the App does not collect personal information for advertising purposes.

If advertising or an advertising SDK is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated as required.

## 9. Analytics and Crash Reporting

If analytics or crash-reporting services are used, they may collect technical information such as:

* App version
* Device model
* Operating system version
* Crash information
* Diagnostic information
* General usage information

Such information is used to maintain, troubleshoot, improve, and secure the App.

If no analytics or crash-reporting service is used, no analytics data is intentionally collected by the App for these purposes.

## 10. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers only when necessary to provide a feature or service used by the App, such as security analysis or technical infrastructure.

Where applicable, third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual obligations.

## 11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should use appropriate caution when sharing QR codes, URLs, contact information, Wi-Fi credentials, or other sensitive information.

## 12. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of identifying or profiling them.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

## 13. Your Choices

You can control certain permissions through your device settings.

You may:

* Deny camera permission
* Remove camera permission after granting it
* Choose whether to use gallery-based scanning
* Delete locally stored scan history
* Delete generated files from your device
* Choose whether to export or share scan information

Some features may not work if the required permission is denied.

## 14. Data Deletion

If information is stored locally by the App, you can generally delete it using the relevant delete or clear functionality provided by the App.

Uninstalling the App may also remove locally stored application data depending on your Android device and system configuration.

If the App introduces an account-based service or server-side personal data storage in the future, appropriate account and data deletion mechanisms will be provided where required.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

You are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Safe QR Code Scanner & Reader is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection.

We do not sell personal information.
