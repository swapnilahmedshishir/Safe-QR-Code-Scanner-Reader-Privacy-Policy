Safe QR Code Scanner & Reader

This Privacy Policy explains how Safe QR Code Scanner & Reader ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

Camera

The App may request camera permission to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera access is used to provide scanning functionality when you use a camera-based scanning feature.

Photos and Images

If you use gallery-based scanning, you may select an image from your device to decode a QR code or barcode.

2. QR Codes and Barcodes

When you scan a QR code or barcode, the App processes the encoded information to provide the requested functionality.

Scanned content may include URLs, text, product identifiers, contact information, Wi-Fi information, telephone numbers, SMS information, email information, or other data encoded in the QR code or barcode.

3. Scan History

The App may provide a scan history feature. Where scan history is stored locally on your device, it remains on your device unless you explicitly export or share it.

4. QR Code Generation

The App allows you to generate QR codes using information that you voluntarily provide, including websites, Wi-Fi networks, contact information, phone numbers, SMS, email, text, and other supported content.

5. Smart Safety Preview / Quishing Protection

The App may inspect URLs contained in scanned QR codes before allowing you to open them. This feature is designed to help users identify potentially suspicious, phishing, scam, or otherwise unsafe URLs.

The App does not guarantee that every malicious, phishing, scam, or unsafe website will be detected. Users should always exercise caution before opening links or entering sensitive information online.

6. Internet Access

The App may use an internet connection for features that require online functionality, including URL safety analysis or other remote services.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. Such services may process limited technical or feature-related information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not sell users' personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for identification or profiling purposes.

10. Your Choices

You can control permissions through your device settings. You may deny or revoke camera permission and delete locally stored scan history where supported by the App.

11. Data Deletion

Information stored locally by the App can generally be deleted through the relevant delete or clear functionality provided by the App.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, applicable laws, or our data practices. The "Last Updated" date will be updated when changes are made.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact us.
